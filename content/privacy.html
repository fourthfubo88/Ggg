Information we process and why

Title searches

As you type in the search box, your browser sends what you've typed directly to Wikidata, a free database run by the Wikimedia Foundation, to suggest matching titles. We don't receive, see or store these searches. The Wikimedia Foundation handles them under its privacy policy.

Availability lookups

When you choose a title on the homepage, your browser asks our lookup service where it's streaming. The service runs on Cloudflare. Each request includes the title's IMDb identifier and, like any web request, your IP address and browser details such as browser type and language. We use these only to answer the request, keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We store availability results by title for about a day, so we don't have to fetch the same title again for the next visitor. These stored results describe the title only, never who asked for it. Our lookup service gets the data from the Streaming Availability API by Movie of the Night; your IP address and browser details aren't passed on to it.

Hosting and security

The site's pages are hosted by GitHub Pages, and our lookup service runs on Cloudflare. Like all hosting providers, they receive your IP address and request details when you visit, and may keep short-term logs to deliver pages, protect against attacks and fix problems. See the GitHub privacy statement and the Cloudflare privacy policy.

The site's fonts are stored on our own hosting, so your browser doesn't contact Google Fonts or other font services when you visit.
