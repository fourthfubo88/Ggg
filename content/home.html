    How it works

    Streaming catalogs differ by country because services license each film and series region by region. A show that left Netflix in the United States may still be on Netflix in Canada, Japan or the UK.

    Streaming Passport checks the catalogs of the major services, including Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, Max, Apple TV, Hulu, Paramount+ and free services like Tubi and Pluto TV, across more than 60 countries. For each country, it also shows which free VPN plans have servers there.

    Catalogs change often, so check the service itself before you subscribe.
