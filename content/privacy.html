Information we process and why

Title searches

As you type in the search box, your browser sends what you've typed directly to Wikidata, a free database operated by the Wikimedia Foundation, to suggest matching titles. We don't receive, see or store these searches. Wikimedia handles these requests under the Wikimedia Foundation privacy policy.

Availability lookups

When you choose a title on the homepage, your browser asks our lookup service where it's streaming. The service runs on Cloudflare Workers. Each request includes the title's IMDb identifier and, like any web request, your IP address and browser details (such as browser type and language). We use this only to answer the request, keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We cache availability results by title for about a day so we don't have to fetch the same title repeatedly. The cache contains information about the title only, never about who asked for it. Our lookup service gets the data from the Streaming Availability API by Movie of the Night; your IP address and browser details aren't passed on to it.

Hosting and security logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and our lookup service runs on Cloudflare. Like most hosting providers, they receive your IP address and request details when you visit, and may keep short-term logs to deliver pages, protect against attacks and fix problems. See the GitHub privacy statement and the Cloudflare privacy policy.

Site statistics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used most. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't fingerprint your device, and doesn't follow you across other websites. We only see totals, such as the number of visits to a page, the countries visits come from and the referring websites.

Advertising

We show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure how ads perform and prevent fraud. With your consent where required, they may also show ads based on your visits to this and other websites ("personalized ads"). Google acts as an independent controller for this processing. To learn more, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Affiliate links

Some links to VPN providers are affiliate links. When you click one, the provider (or an affiliate network working for it) may set a cookie or record the click so it can tell you came from our site. If you then buy a paid plan, it pays us a commission. We receive reports showing numbers of clicks and sales, not your name, email address or payment details. See our affiliate disclosure.
